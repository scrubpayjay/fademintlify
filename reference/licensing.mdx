Your access to the Fade Quant Partner API is governed by a data license that defines permitted uses clearly and explicitly. Before building on top of Fade data, take a few minutes to understand what your license grants you, what it does not cover, and how to pursue additional rights if your use case requires them.

<Warning>
  **Redistribution and resale of API responses — in whole or in part — require a separate written agreement with Sportshack.** Publishing Fade forecasts, probabilities, or any derivative of API response data to external audiences without a publisher license is a violation of your usage terms.
</Warning>

---

## Standard License: Internal Analytical Use

The Quant Partner API subscription — available at **$1,500/month** or **$5,000/season** — grants you a license for **internal analytical use only**. This means you and your authorized team members may:

- Query the API and store responses in your own internal systems.
- Build analytical models, dashboards, and research tools for your own decision-making.
- Evaluate forecast performance and calibration against historical grades.
- Incorporate Fade probabilities into your own proprietary research workflows.

All use is subject to the terms agreed upon at subscription.

## What's Not Included

The Fade API is a **Sportshack analytics product**. It does not carry, and your license does not grant access to, any of the following:

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Venue Prices, Bids, and Asks" icon="dollar-sign">
    The API contains no current or historical price data from any venue. There are no bid prices, ask prices, mid prices, last-traded prices, or mark prices in any response object.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Order Books and Liquidity Data" icon="book">
    No order book snapshots, depth-of-market data, open interest, or liquidity metrics are available through the API.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trade History and Volume" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    No trade records, fill histories, contract volumes, or notional value data are present in any response.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Venue Account or Position Data" icon="lock">
    The API has no connection to any venue account. It cannot read balances, positions, open orders, or settlement records.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Field-Licensing Classification: Venue Identifiers

Fields such as `venue`, `venue_market_id`, and `event_ticker` appear on Market and Forecast objects. These fields are **navigational references only** — they allow you to look up the corresponding contract on the originating venue's website or API. They do not constitute a license to scrape, mirror, or redistribute venue data. Any use of venue data beyond navigating to a publicly accessible URL is governed by that venue's own terms of service.

## What Requires a Separate Agreement

The following uses are **not permitted** under the standard internal-use license. Each requires a separate written agreement with Sportshack before any use begins:

- Publishing Fade forecasts or `p_yes` values on a public website, app, or platform.
- Distributing API response data to third parties, paying or non-paying.
- Reselling access to Fade data as part of a data product or subscription service.
- White-labeling or re-branding Fade outputs for commercial distribution.
- Embedding Fade probabilities in any media product intended for external audiences.

## Publisher License Path

If you are a media company, data distributor, or platform operator interested in publishing Fade probabilities to an external audience, contact Sportshack to discuss publisher licensing terms.

Send an email to **sportshackdfs@gmail.com** with the subject line:

> **Sportshack Publisher License**

Include a brief description of your platform, intended audience size, and how you plan to use the data. A member of the Sportshack team will respond with available licensing structures.
