Fade is a sports probability analytics platform built by Sportshack that identifies disagreements between a proprietary forecasting model and the implied probabilities derived from current market odds. When the model and the market diverge meaningfully, Fade surfaces that signal — called a **Reality Engine** disagreement — so analysts, researchers, and quantitative teams can study, back-test, and evaluate it within their own workflows. Sportshack is a strictly analytics-only platform: no wagering is facilitated, no funds are held, and no bets are processed. All outputs are probabilities, not recommendations.

## What Problems Fade Solves

Sports probability markets are noisy. Line movements, sharp action, public sentiment, and book margin all blur the signal embedded in the odds. Fade's Reality Engine strips out book margin through a devigging process to recover the market's implied fair probability, then compares that figure against the model's own forecast. The resulting disagreement score gives you a clean, timestamped, provenance-tracked signal that you can evaluate over time with a complete graded record — including misses.

## Who Uses Fade

Fade is designed for three primary audiences:

- **Independent analysts and researchers** who want a structured, auditable dataset of model-vs-market disagreements to study forecast calibration and market efficiency.
- **Quantitative teams** at sports analytics firms who need a reliable feed of timestamped probabilities and grades they can pipe into their own models or dashboards.
- **Data-driven sports professionals** who want daily visibility into where a systematic model disagrees with consensus market pricing, across MLB and NFL today, with NBA, NHL, MMA, Soccer, Tennis, and Golf coming soon.
