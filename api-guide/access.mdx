Access to the Quant Partner API is granted through a concierge onboarding process — there is no self-serve sign-up flow. Sportshack reviews each request individually to confirm intended use, issue the right license tier, and deliver a customer-named key through a secure channel. The three steps below cover everything you need to go from inquiry to your first authenticated request.

<Note>
  Each API key is independently rate-limited and independently revocable. If your team needs multiple keys — for example, to isolate production and development traffic — mention that during the contact step and Sportshack will provision them separately.
</Note>

## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Sportshack">
    Send an email to [sportshackdfs@gmail.com](mailto:sportshackdfs@gmail.com) with the subject line **"Quant Partner API Access Request"**. Include the following in your message:

    - Your full name
    - Company or organization name
    - Work email address
    - Intended internal use (for example: "backtesting model calibration" or "daily forecast dashboard")
    - Preferred license term: **monthly** ($1,500/month) or **seasonal** ($5,000/season)
    - Expected request volume per day or per minute, if known

    Sportshack will respond to confirm eligibility and provide next steps for payment. You do not need an existing Sportshack account to apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Licensing and Payment">
    Once Sportshack confirms your request, you will receive either a **Stripe Payment Link** or an **invoice** depending on your preference. Pricing is:

    | Term | Price |
    |---|---|
    | Monthly | $1,500 / month |
    | Seasonal | $5,000 / season |

    Payment covers internal analytical use of the Fade probability feed. It does **not** automatically create or activate an API key — Sportshack provisions keys manually after payment is confirmed, which allows each key to be named, scoped, and rate-limited to your account specifically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive and Verify Your Key">
    After payment is confirmed, Sportshack delivers your API key **once** through a secure channel. The key name will reflect your account or organization so you can identify it in logs and support requests.

    To verify activation, make a request to `GET /api/v1/manifest` with your key in the `Authorization` header. Sportshack monitors for this first authenticated request as part of the activation handshake.

    ```bash
    curl -s https://fade.sportshack.ai/api/v1/manifest \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer fade_live_YOUR_KEY_HERE"
    ```

    A `200 OK` response confirms your key is active and your account is ready. If you receive a `401`, contact [sportshackdfs@gmail.com](mailto:sportshackdfs@gmail.com) — the key may still be in the provisioning queue.

    Store your key in a secrets manager or environment variable immediately. Sportshack stores only a **hash** of your key server-side and cannot retrieve the plaintext value after delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you need **publisher or redistribution rights** — for example, to embed Fade probability data in a product or service delivered to third parties — that requires a separate written agreement beyond the standard analytical license. Contact [sportshackdfs@gmail.com](mailto:sportshackdfs@gmail.com) with the subject **"Sportshack Publisher License"** to start that conversation.
</Tip>
