The Fade is Sportshack's analytics product: a daily probability board that shows you exactly where the model diverges from the market, backed by a complete graded record that always includes misses. For teams building internal research tools, the Quant Partner API delivers those same forecasts with full provenance, versioned model identifiers, and bulk export capability.

## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Sportshack Account">
    Sign up at [sportshack.ai](https://sportshack.ai/start?ref=fade) to access the Reality Engine board and today's free daily slate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request API Access">
    Contact Sportshack at [fade.sportshack.ai/developers/access](https://fade.sportshack.ai/developers/access) with your use case, company, and preferred license term. Starting at $1,500/month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your API Key">
    After scope and terms are confirmed, your customer-named key is delivered once through a secure channel — shown once, never stored in plaintext.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Querying">
    Pass your key as a Bearer token and start pulling forecasts, grades, and model history. See the [API Quickstart](/guides/quickstart-api) for working examples.
  </Step>
</Steps>
